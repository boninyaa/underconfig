what is underconfig?

a lightweight configurator for Team Fortress 2 based on solely core web technologies. the whole thing runs on a single javascript file.

how do i use it?

click the "autoexec.cfg" button and download the .cfg file, then put it into your cfg directory before launching TF2.

on windows, your cfg folder is typically located in

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Team Fortress 2\tf\cfg

on linux (non-flatpak), your cfg folder is typically located in

/home/yourusername/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/Team Fortress 2/tf/cfg
